# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 1 September 2026

Bank Statement Parser is built on a strict **zero-telemetry, zero-cookie** architecture:

1. **No External Data Transmission:** The Bank Statement Parser software operates entirely on the user's infrastructure. No document content, transaction records, or telemetry data are ever collected or transmitted.
2. **No Tracking Cookies:** This documentation website does not use tracking cookies, analytics pixels, or third-party behavioral scripts.
3. **Data Protection Compliance:** Fully compliant with GDPR, UK Data Protection Act 2018, and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).
