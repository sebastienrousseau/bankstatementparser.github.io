Enterprise Use Cases & Deployment Patterns

High-throughput statement extraction powers critical automated financial backends.

Use Case 1

Corporate Treasury Automation

Consolidate disparate statements from 20+ global banking partners into a unified daily cash position. Automatically feeds ISO 20022 camt.053 feeds into SAP, Oracle NetSuite, and Kyriba TMS.

Use Case 2

Automated Bank Reconciliation

Match line-item transaction deposits against general ledger invoices. The deterministic tokenizer normalizes reference codes and cleans payee descriptions for 99.8% match rates.

Use Case 3

Fintech SME Credit Underwriting

Ingest 12–24 months of applicant PDF statements in milliseconds. Calculate monthly net operating cashflows, recurring subscription burn, and overdraft frequency with zero human keying.

Use Case 4

Forensic Audit & Sanctions Screening

Extract all counterparty names, IBANs, and transaction narratives across thousands of historic statements. Stream clean transaction metadata directly into anti-money laundering (AML) screening graphs.
