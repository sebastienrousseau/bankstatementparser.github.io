## Introduction

Bank Statement Parser ("we", "us", "our") is an open-source Python library. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and use information when you visit our website at bankstatementparser.com.

The Bank Statement Parser library itself processes all data locally on your machine. No financial data, personal data, or bank statement content is ever transmitted to our servers or any third party.

## Information We Collect

### Website Analytics

We use the following analytics services to understand how visitors use our website:

- **Google Analytics 4** (Measurement ID: G-FL9DEBFHN1) -- collects anonymised usage data including pages visited, time on site, and referral sources. Google Analytics uses cookies. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Microsoft Clarity** -- records anonymised session replays including mouse movements, clicks, and scrolls to help us improve the user experience. Clarity does not collect personal data. See [Microsoft's Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

### Contact Form

If you submit our contact form (powered by Formspree), we collect your name, email address, and message content. This data is processed by Formspree and stored according to their [privacy policy](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/).

## Data We Do Not Collect

- We do not collect or process any bank statement data.
- We do not use cookies for advertising or tracking beyond the analytics services listed above.
- We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.
- We do not collect payment information.

## Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, you have the following rights:

- **Right of access** -- request a copy of the data we hold about you.
- **Right to rectification** -- request correction of inaccurate data.
- **Right to erasure** -- request deletion of your personal data.
- **Right to restrict processing** -- request that we limit how we use your data.
- **Right to data portability** -- request your data in a machine-readable format.
- **Right to object** -- object to processing of your personal data.

To exercise any of these rights, please use the contact details at the bottom of this page.

## Data Retention

- **Analytics data** is retained according to Google and Microsoft's standard retention periods (14 months for Google Analytics, 30 days for Microsoft Clarity session recordings).
- **Contact form submissions** are retained for the duration necessary to respond to your enquiry, then deleted.

## Cookies

This website uses the following cookies:

| Cookie | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| _ga, _ga_* | Google Analytics | Analytics tracking | 2 years |
| MUID, CLID | Microsoft Clarity | Session tracking | 1 year |

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. The website will continue to function without cookies.

## Children's Privacy

Our website and library are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Last updated: April 2026.
