Parser Bayanin Banki yana aiwatar da ayyukan kuɗi na ainihi na duniya: ƙaura MT940-zuwa-CAMT don ƙungiyoyin baitulmali, sulhu ta atomatik, bin bututun mai tare da sake gyara PII, shigar SFTP, haɓakar banki da yawa, da amintaccen sarrafa tsari na ZIP.

## Baitul mali: MT940 zuwa CAMT.053 Hijira

**Sakamako:** Kiran API guda ɗaya yana ɗaukar duka MT940 da CAMT.053 yayin taga ƙaura na SWIFT (Nuwamba 2025-Nuwamba 2028), yana kawar da buƙatar raba bututun ɓarna.

Ƙungiyoyin baitulmali a duk duniya suna ƙaura daga MT940 zuwa CAMT.053 gabanin wa'adin ƙarshe na watan Nuwamba 2027 SWIFT. Fassarar Bayanin Banki yana sarrafa nau'ikan nau'ikan biyu tare da API guda ɗaya, yana mai da canji mara kyau.

```python
from bankstatementparser import create_parser, detect_statement_format

# Process both MT940 and CAMT.053 with the same code
for file in daily_statement_files:
    fmt = detect_statement_format(file)
    parser = create_parser(file, fmt)
    df = parser.parse()
    load_to_treasury_system(df)
```

## Sasantawa ta atomatik

**Sakamako:** Tsarin-Agnostic DataFrames tare da ginanniyar haɓakawa suna rage ƙoƙarin daidaita aikin hannu da kama shigarwar kwafi kafin su kai ga littafan ku.

Rarraba bayanan banki kuma daidaita da bayanan ciki ta atomatik. Fitowar DataFrame mai haɗin kai yana yin tsarin dabaru na sulhu-agnostic.

```python
from bankstatementparser import CamtParser, Deduplicator

parser = CamtParser("bank_statement.xml")
bank_txns = parser.parse()

# Deduplicate before reconciliation
dedup = Deduplicator()
result = dedup.deduplicate(dedup.from_dataframe(bank_txns))
clean_txns = result.unique_transactions

# Match against internal records
unmatched = reconcile(clean_txns, internal_ledger)
```

## Bibiya da Bututun Audit

**Sakamako:** Ƙaddara fitarwa da sabuntawar PII ta atomatik suna samar da shirye-shiryen rajistan ayyukan tantancewa waɗanda ke gamsar da buƙatun sake fasalin tsari ba tare da ƙarin kayan aiki ba.

Gina bututun da aka shirya a shirye tare da gyara PII da fitarwa mai ƙima. Kowane gudu yana samar da sakamako iri ɗaya don shigarwa iri ɗaya, gamsassun buƙatun sake fasalin tsari.

```python
from bankstatementparser import CamtParser

parser = CamtParser("statement.xml")

# Stream with PII redacted for audit logs
for txn in parser.parse_streaming(redact_pii=True):
    audit_log.write(txn)

# Export full data for secure internal processing
parser.export_csv("archive/statement.csv")
```

## SFTP-zuwa-DataFrame Workflows

**Sakamako:** Rarraba kai tsaye daga bytes tare da sifilin faifai I/O, dacewa da asali cikin SFTP da ayyukan haɗin banki da ke tafiyar da API.

Yawancin bankuna suna ba da sanarwa ta hanyar SFTP. Rarraba kai tsaye daga bytes ba tare da rubuta zuwa faifai ba.

```python
from bankstatementparser import CamtParser

xml_bytes = sftp_client.read("daily_statement.xml")
parser = CamtParser.from_bytes(xml_bytes, source_name="daily.xml")
df = parser.parse()
```

## Haɗin Babban Banki

**Sakamako:** Daidaitawar tantancewa a cikin HSBC (CAMT), Barclays (MT940), Revolut (CSV), da Wise (OFX) suna samar da daidaitattun saitin bayanai guda ɗaya a cikin kira ɗaya.

Ƙirƙirar bayanai daga bankuna da yawa ta amfani da tsari daban-daban zuwa saitin bayanai guda ɗaya na al'ada.

```python
from bankstatementparser import parse_files_parallel

results = parse_files_parallel([
    "hsbc/camt053.xml",
    "barclays/mt940.sta",
    "revolut/transactions.csv",
    "wise/statement.ofx",
])

all_transactions = pd.concat([r.transactions for r in results if r.status == "success"])
```

## Gudanar da Batch tare da Ajiyayyen ZIP

**Sakamako:** Kariyar bam na ZIP da aka gina a ciki (iyakar rabo 100:1, iyakar shigarwa 10 MB, rufaffen shigarwar shigarwa) yana ba ku damar aiwatar da bayanan bayanan wata-wata lafiya.

Tsara bayanan da aka zub da su cikin aminci tare da ginanniyar kariyar bam na ZIP.

```python
from bankstatementparser import iter_secure_xml_entries, CamtParser

for entry in iter_secure_xml_entries("monthly_statements.zip"):
    parser = CamtParser.from_bytes(entry.xml_bytes, source_name=entry.source_name)
    df = parser.parse()
    save_to_warehouse(entry.source_name, df)
```
