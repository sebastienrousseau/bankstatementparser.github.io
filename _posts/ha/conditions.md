## Yarjejeniyar zuwa Sharuɗɗan

Ta hanyar shiga bankstatementparser.com ("Shafin Yanar Gizo") da kuma amfani da ɗakin karatu na Bayanin Banki ("Software"), kun yarda ku ɗaure ku da waɗannan Sharuɗɗan Amfani. Idan ba ku yarda ba, don Allah kar a yi amfani da Yanar Gizo ko Software.

## Lasisin Budaddiyar Madogararsa

An fito da ɗakin karatu na Bayanan Bayanin Banki a ƙarƙashin [Lasisi na Apache 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). Kuna iya amfani, gyara, da rarraba software daidai da wannan lasisi. Ana samun cikakken rubutun lasisi a cikin [majigin GitHub](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/bankstatementparser/blob/main/LICENSE).

## Amfani da Yanar Gizo

Kuna iya amfani da gidan yanar gizon don dalilai na halal kawai. Kun yarda ba:

- Ƙoƙarin samun shiga mara izini zuwa kowane ɓangare na Gidan Yanar Gizo.
- Yi amfani da gidan yanar gizon don aika lambar mugaye ko gudanar da hare-haren hana sabis.
- Maimaita, kwafi, ko sake siyar da kowane yanki na gidan yanar gizon ba tare da rubutaccen izini ba.

## Amfani da Software

An tanadar da software don tantance fayilolin bayanin banki a cikin gida akan tsarin ku. Kai kaɗai ke da alhakin:

- Tabbatar da amfani da software ɗin ya bi ƙa'idodi da ƙa'idodi.
- daidaito da amincin bayanan da software ke sarrafa.
- Tsare duk wani mahimman bayanan kuɗi da aka sarrafa ta amfani da software.

## Dukiyar Hankali

Sunan Fassarar Bayanin Banki, tambari, da abun cikin gidan yanar gizon mallakar Sebastien Rousseau ne. Lambar tushen software tana da lasisi ƙarƙashin Apache 2.0 kamar yadda aka bayyana a sama.
